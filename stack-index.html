Evidence, not theatre

Every material claim needs a trail.

  1. 01

    Name the source

    Popularity signals carry their platform, metric, scope, and observation date. A download is not a star, and neither is performance.

  2. 02

    Record the conditions

    Verified performance belongs to an exact stack, suite, protocol, task count, trial count, version set, and source record.

  3. 03

    Keep missing data visible

    When a comparison is not like-for-like, Stack Index says so. It does not fill gaps with a confident-looking score.
