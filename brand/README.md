# Openly Useful Brand Kit

The Openly Useful identity turns the idea of a comfortable sharing ecosystem into a simple, implementable world.

## Core metaphor

The **Open Shell** is a Unix shell, an open doorway, and a safe boundary with room left for participation. Every expression should reinforce that balance: open but not exposed, capable but not intimidating, nostalgic but not frozen in the past.

## Emotional direction

Think shared Unix workstation, public library computer room, a carefully maintained README, a corkboard of local knowledge, and patient people teaching one another. Use beige hardware, recycled paper, soft plants, green task lamps, wood, matte plastic, and restrained CRT texture.

Nostalgia comes from **shared rituals**, not vintage decoration. Never reduce contrast, use illegible pixel type, add faux scan-line damage, or copy Linux/Tux, BSD, Roblox, or another project’s mascot language.

## Character system

The Shellfolk are bracket-derived modular maintainers. Favor scenes with two or more characters pairing, reviewing, repairing, carrying, or teaching. Their posture is neutral and useful—not heroic, collectible, or game-like. Keep the head/body geometry, rounded joints, bracket visor, and palette consistent.

## Production rules

- Preserve clear space of one mark width around the logo.
- Minimum digital mark size: 20 px. Minimum print size: 6 mm.
- Use Terminal green on Shell; use the reverse mark on Ink or Terminal.
- Do not rotate, close, stretch, circle, add a drop shadow to, or recolor the mark.
- Do not place the character where a system error, warning, or destructive action needs undivided attention.
- Use the exact tagline: **Useful things, openly made.**
