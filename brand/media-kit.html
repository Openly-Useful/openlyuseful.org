Usage rules

Keep it recognizably ours

The logo itself never adapts to a platform. Only the canvas, safe zone, and surrounding composition adapt.

Use the supplied file. Do not rebuild the O, U, eyes, wordmark, or tagline.
Protect the clear space. Keep at least one inner-U width around the institutional mark.
Choose primary or reverse. Never add outlines, shadows, gradients, masks, or automatic recoloring.
