01 · Brand idea

The Open Shell

A Unix shell. An open doorway. A protective boundary that leaves room for participation. The mark makes our promise visible: open does not have to mean exposed, and safe does not have to mean closed.

04 · Character

Shellfolk

Small modular maintainers who pair, review, carry, repair, and share. Their geometry comes from the mark; their warmth comes from working together.

Never heroes. Never gatekeepers. Always one more chair.
