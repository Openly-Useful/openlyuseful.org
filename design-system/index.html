01 · Brand idea

The Open Monitor

The outer O is an open, shared environment. The inner U is useful work, the user, and you. Two cursor-like eyes turn that relationship into a welcoming monitor face: capable, inspectable, and ready for one more person.

$ openly-useful --share

Optical proof · production pixels

Each eye shares its centerline with a U stem. The four-unit eye gap and 2.5-unit lower counter protect the face at small sizes.

04 · Character

Monitorfolk

Small modular maintainers who pair, review, carry, repair, and share. Every screen repeats the O/U face, while antennas, colors, and workwear give the crew room to vary.

Never heroes. Never gatekeepers. Always one more chair.
